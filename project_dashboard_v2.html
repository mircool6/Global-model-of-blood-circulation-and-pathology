🔬 Blade Geometry Impact & PrePoMax FEA

📊 Blade Geometry & Mesh Impact on Hemodynamics

📐 Mesh Independence: Transitioning from coarse (89k DOF) to fine mesh (276k DOF) refines the results. Torque magnitude increases from ~0.35 to 0.464 N·mm.

⚙️ Blade Count (1, 2, 3 blades): Increasing the number of blades increases Torque and Hemolysis (Dissipation Rate), while decreasing Thrombosis risk (Stagnant Volume drops from 0.27% to 0.19%).

🔄 Stagger Mesh Configuration: The staggered geometry radically changes the flow profile, resulting in near-zero torque and extremely low hemolysis and thrombosis risks compared to baseline.

Risk Register & Milestone Gates

🔴 Velocity noise / convection dominance

Spatial oscillations in VTK at high Re. Mesh tangling near sharp branch corners.

Fix: SUPG stabilization in F_fluid + inverse-volume Laplacian ALE stiffening.

🔴 Added-mass instability (ρ_fluid ≈ ρ_solid)

1060 vs 1100 kg/m³. Partitioned FSI inherently unstable.

Mitigated: Zero-order predictor + DT=2.5e-4. Monitor at >200 steps.

⚠ N3 stagger fine mesh: 239k cells

Exceeds 200k cell limit set for the sweep study.

Fix: Reduce mesh size factor in Gmsh for N3-stagger-fine case.

⚠ 2D geometry: cantilever bending

Side branches bend instead of radially expanding. Sharp corners → singularities.

Plan: Accepted for Phase 4. Smooth geometry + 3D mesh in Phase 6.
