✅ Phase 0: Engine Development Jul 2026

FreeCAD parametrization + 9 case generator. Outputs: .step + .msh files.

✅ Phase 1: Geometry & Mesh Aug 2026

Parametric 2D aorta (3 branches) + FSI mesh (3683 nodes, 7792 elems). Tags: inlet, outlet_1–3, wall, interface.

✅ Phase 2: Rigid CFD (Pulsatile) Aug–Sep 2026

IPCS + BDF2 solvers, Windkessel RC outlets. Time-varying elastance heart model. Mass error < 1.5%.

✅ Phase 3: Clinical Data & Scenarios Sep 2026

3.1 WHO/GBD Data Parsing: Automated collection of cardiovascular risk factors from public databases.

3.2 NPZ Scenario Export: 20 clinical boundary condition datasets (baseline, HTN, DM, stenosis, clot, combinations).

3.3 Markov Progression Analysis: Disease state transitions, mortality modeling, clinical progression pathways. (markov_cfd_analysis.py)

🔄 Phase 4: FSI Solver (In Progress) Oct–Dec 2026

Partitioned ALE FSI (BDF2 fluid + plane-strain elasticity). Aitken relaxation ω ∈ [0.03, 0.80]. Zero-order predictor. VTK output every 10 steps.

🔴 Phase 5: Pathological Scenario Runs Nov–Dec 2026

Run 3+ clinical scenarios: baseline, hypertension, severe clot. Extract WSS, pressure, wall displacement. Comparison plots. Integrate Markov predictions.

🔴 Phase 6: Stabilization & 3D Dec 2026–Feb 2027

🔴 Phase 7: Visualization & Reports Feb–Mar 2027

🔴 Phase 8: Production FSI (MFEM) Mar–Aug 2027

🔴 HIGH: FSI Divergence on Pathological Cases (Phase 5)

Aitken relaxation may fail on severe stenosis / clot scenarios. Markov predictions may show low survival probability, requiring solver robustness. Mitigation: pre-test with mild stenosis; use adaptive time-stepping.

🟡 MEDIUM: Slow FSI Convergence

Aitken ω may saturate at 0.80, causing slow inner loops. Blocks Phase 5 completion. Mitigation: implement Anderson mixing as fallback; profile solver performance.

🟡 MEDIUM: Markov Data Completeness

WHO/GBD datasets may have gaps or outdated assumptions. Markov transition matrices may not reflect true clinical progression. Mitigation: validate against clinical literature; refine with real patient cohorts.

🟡 MEDIUM: 3D Mesh Quality (Phase 6/8)

Real CT/MRI geometries may have sharp features, singularities. Mitigation: validate mesh early with quality metrics; smooth in preprocessing.

🟡 MEDIUM: MFEM Monolithic Migration (Phase 8)

Porting from partitioned DolfinX to monolithic MFEM is risky. Markov-informed scenario selection may need solver tuning. Mitigation: benchmark on 2D first; stage migration carefully.
