🔴 HIGH: FSI Divergence on Pathological Cases (Phase 5)

Aitken relaxation may fail on severe stenosis / clot scenarios. Mitigation: pre-test with mild stenosis; use adaptive time-stepping.

🟡 MEDIUM: Slow FSI Convergence

Aitken ω may saturate at 0.80, causing slow inner loops. Mitigation: implement Anderson mixing as fallback.

🟡 MEDIUM: 3D Mesh Quality (Phase 8)

Real CT/MRI geometries may have sharp features, singularities. Mitigation: validate mesh early with quality metrics; smooth in preprocessing.

🟡 MEDIUM: MFEM Monolithic Solver Instability

Migrating from partitioned DolfinX to monolithic MFEM is risky. Mitigation: benchmark on 2D first; stage migration carefully.
